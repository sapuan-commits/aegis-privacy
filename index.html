Aegis – Privacy Policy

App: Aegis (package top.nexahost.aegis) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who this app is for

Aegis is a private tool with a single user: its owner. It is used to review and approve requests from the owner's own systems. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the app processes

  • Sign-in identity: the owner's email address, as confirmed by the sign-in service the owner uses to protect their own servers. The sign-in tokens are stored on the device, encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Device key: a cryptographic key created on the device to confirm approvals. The private key never leaves the device; only its public part is registered with the owner's servers.
  • Push endpoint: an address used to deliver notifications to this device through the owner's own notification service. Notifications contain no request content; the app fetches details itself after sign-in.
  • Approval data: requests, decisions and notes that the app reads from and sends to the owner's own servers.
  • Biometrics: fingerprint or screen-lock checks are handled entirely by Android on the device. The app never receives or stores biometric data.

Where data goes

The app exchanges data with the owner's own servers over encrypted connections. Cloudflare processes sign-in and connection data on the owner's behalf. Google Play distributes and updates the app.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. No data is sold, and no data is shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Retention and deletion

Signing out deletes the sign-in tokens from the device and removes the device's push registration. Device-key registrations can be revoked. Decisions, notes and audit records are retained as an approval history until the owner requests their deletion. Uninstalling the app deletes the data the app stored on the device; the app does not use Android cloud backup.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the owner through the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
